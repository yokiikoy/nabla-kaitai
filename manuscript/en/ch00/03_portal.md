This book is published for free. Obvious typo-level corrections are also accepted through GitHub Issues.

> <strong>Note</strong> (on the name)  
> Incidentally, the term *covector space*, or *Co-Vector Space*, is not especially common. In mathematics, it is more usual to say *dual space* and related terms.

### Discord Server

As a small experiment, I have opened a Discord server as a slightly more closed place where people can bring unorganized ideas and elementary questions that are hard to throw onto public social media.

The plan is to develop it gradually. At present, it is honestly close to an “empty room.” I hope it can become a place where people interested in science and engineering can gather at a comfortable distance, not limited to the contents of this book.

If you are interested, please take a look through the portal site.
