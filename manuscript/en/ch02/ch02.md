# Chapter 2: What Is Area? — The Sign Rule Hidden in Parallelepipeds

### §2.0 Measuring Devices, Area, Volume, and Length

In Chapter 1, we defined $dx$, $dy$, and $dz$ as row vectors ($1$-forms) that eat one vector and return a scalar, and we reread $\int f\,dx$ as the limit of matrix actions. In this chapter we extend that framework and <strong>discover area-measuring devices</strong> ($2$-forms) that eat two vectors and <strong>volume-measuring devices</strong> ($3$-forms) that eat three, built from the same parts $dx$, $dy$, and $dz$.
