# Preface: To the Lucky, the Suffering, and the Rebels

**To the college freshmen:** You're lucky. Your brain hasn't been poisoned yet. Close the textbook. It's just a rulebook for making simple things pedantic. Let's keep your intuition intact.

**To those suffering through Vector Analysis:** I know your pain. It's not your fault. You've been handed a manual written in 19th-century Latin for a legacy standard. It's a terrible way to learn. Let's just build a better one.

**To those lost in the fog of Differential Forms and Tensor Analysis:** This is the answer you've been looking for. No more vague analogies or hiding behind jargon. We are going to unmask the beast with some actual algebra.

**And to the mathematicians:** Screw you. I'm not here to admire the beautiful scaffolding; I'm here to build a house that stays upright. My "proofs" are in the working code and in describing real phenomena. The universe runs on operations, not on lemmas.

**The Bottom Line:** My degree says "Physics," but I'm a programmer now — some might say a hacker. This book is "badly behaved." It's fast, it's dirty, and it works.

Welcome to the Matrix Bypass.
