## Important translation decisions

### 1. $\mathbf{v}$ in parameter components

The section explicitly distinguishes $\mathbf{v}=\begin{pmatrix}\Delta r\\\Delta\theta\\\Delta z\end{pmatrix}$ from Cartesian $\begin{pmatrix}\Delta x\\\Delta y\\\Delta z\end{pmatrix}$ in §1.0–§1.2. This warning is kept verbatim in spirit.

### 2. Row versus column for $dx$

The parenthetical clarifies that the cylindrical row is not “the Cartesian row stood up as a column on the right,” matching the Japanese warning about 縦ベクトル.

### 3. Dimensional analysis

The distinction between the numerical coefficient $-1$ and the entry $-r\sin\theta$ carrying length dimension is preserved — central to §1.5.2.

### 4. `linear form` closing

§1.5.3 ends with the measuring-device / coordinate-distortion property of linear forms, setting up later chapters.
