## Important Translation Decisions

### 1. `parameter space` and `physical space`

The Japanese distinguishes 実空間 and パラメータ空間. The English draft uses `physical space` and `parameter space` consistently.

### 2. Pullback terminology

引き戻し is translated as `pullback`. The section explicitly introduces `Phi^*(dx)` in this language, matching the Chapter 4 terminology already foreshadowed in §1.2.

### 3. Avoiding curved-grid-first explanations

The draft preserves the book's preferred route: first map a small parameter-space step into physical space, then measure its image algebraically. Curved-grid intuition is acknowledged as correct but not used as the primary explanation.

### 4. Two-place viewpoint

The phrase `There are two places, physical space and parameter space, and between them there is a translation rule Phi` preserves the author's intended cognitive reset: do not think only of curved coordinate axes growing inside physical space.
