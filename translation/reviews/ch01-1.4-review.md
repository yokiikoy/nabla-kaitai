## Important translation decisions

### 1. `parameter space` and `physical space`

The Japanese distinguishes 実空間 and パラメータ空間. English uses `physical space` and `parameter space` consistently.

### 2. Pullback terminology

引き戻し → `pullback`; `Phi^*(dx)` matches Chapter 4 foreshadowing in §1.2.

### 3. Algebraic procedure first

Small parameter-space step → map to physical space → measure with `dx`; curved-grid intuition acknowledged but secondary.

### 4. Two-place viewpoint

`There are two places, physical space and parameter space, and between them there is a translation rule Phi` preserves the author's cognitive reset.
